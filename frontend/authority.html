Reporting after an SLA breach

Between departments

Normally a file that nobody in a department resolves goes straight to the Deputy Commissioner. Here the District Administrator can decide that a department reports to another department first: the file lands on that department's head officer, who gets a fresh SLA window. Rules can chain (A → B → C). Departments without a rule go to the Deputy Commissioner, then the CMO.

💾 Move data between environments

Your citizens, complaints, officers, and settings live in this browser's storage — they don't travel with the zip file, and a different folder, port, or the live Netlify site each start empty. Export everything here, then import it wherever you want the same data to appear.

Importing replaces everything currently in this browser — citizens, complaints, officers, MCs, and passwords — with the file's contents.

🛠️ Site maintenance

Take the site offline

Citizens, officers, and councillors will see a "temporarily unavailable" page instead of their dashboards. Administration sign-in always stays reachable so you can turn it back on.

Leave blank to keep the site closed until you switch it back on yourself.
