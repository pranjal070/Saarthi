The Saarthi promise

1 · File it

Describe the issue, pin it on the map, attach a photo, and submit for an instant application number.

2 · It reaches the desk

Lands directly on the designated officer's dashboard — DSP, Xen, EO — of the concerned department.

3 · Officer acts

The officer reviews, assigns a field officer with contact details, and records every remark on file.

4 · Resolved in 36h

Or the file auto-escalates to the higher official. You rate the work, or reopen it.
